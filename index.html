How it Works

Three simple steps from cough to clear guidance.

1

Record the Cough

Hold your phone near the child and record a short cough sample using the built-in microphone.

2

On-Device Analysis

The AI evaluates pitch, duration, and barking characteristics privately on your device.

3

Get Clear Results

Receive a likelihood score, feature breakdown, and practical next-step guidance.

About the Project

CroupGuard was created to explore how accessible technology can help families better understand concerning coughs, especially in communities where specialist care or clear health information can be harder to reach.

The project focuses on making useful guidance available in languages that matter to many families — including Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya — while keeping privacy at the center through on-device processing.

It began as a student initiative driven by the belief that practical health tools should not be limited by language barriers or limited resources. The goal is to support caregivers with clearer information so they can make more confident decisions and seek timely care when needed.
