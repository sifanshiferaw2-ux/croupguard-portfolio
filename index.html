A student project exploring how privacy-first AI and multilingual design can help families better understand concerning coughs — especially where language or resource barriers make clear information harder to find.

Why I Built This

When I was younger, my little brother had croup three times. Each time we only realized how serious it was in the final stages. Once we were eating lunch and he suddenly struggled to breathe. We almost lost him. That experience is why I created CroupGuard — to help other families recognize concerning coughs earlier, especially in communities with limited access to information.

About This Project

CroupGuard is a student project created by Sifan Shiferaw. It started from a personal family experience and grew into a tool focused on early awareness, privacy, and language accessibility. It is still a prototype and not a medical device. The goal is to keep improving it so more families can benefit.

Contact: sifanshiferaw2@gmail.com

Privacy Note

This prototype does not collect or store personal health data on a server. Feedback messages are sent privately to the creator. Only a simple count of how many people submitted feedback is kept public. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.
