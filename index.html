Core Features

Designed as a research-oriented prototype focused on accessibility and privacy.

On-Device AI Analysis

Records a short cough and estimates the likelihood of croup-like acoustic features (barking quality, pitch, duration) using local processing.

Multilingual Support

Full interface planned for Amharic, Afaan Oromoo (Oromiffa), Tigrinya, English, and other major languages to improve health equity.

Privacy-First Design

Audio stays on the device by default. No raw recordings are uploaded unless the user explicitly chooses to contribute data.

Symptom Tracking

Ability to log multiple recordings over time and view trends, with option to export a simple summary for a doctor.

Smart Triage Guidance

Quick questions (age, fever, breathing difficulty) paired with evidence-based next-step suggestions — always with strong medical disclaimers.

Educational Content

Clear explanations of what croup is, why the cough sounds distinctive, and important red-flag warning signs.

About This Project

CroupGuard is an independent student portfolio project. It explores how on-device audio classification and thoughtful multilingual design could help caregivers better understand concerning coughs — especially the distinctive barking cough associated with croup.

The project was created to practice product thinking, AI concept design, and accessible interface planning, with particular attention to language support for Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya speakers.

This is a concept / prototype, not a finished medical product. No real clinical validation has been performed.

Important Medical Disclaimer:
CroupGuard is a student portfolio concept only. It is not a medical device, not clinically validated, and must never be used to diagnose or make real medical decisions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for any health concerns, especially breathing difficulties in children.
