Making early cough guidance
more accessible

A student project exploring how privacy-first design and multilingual support can help families better understand concerning coughs.

Why I Built This

My family and I never recognized the early symptoms of croup. As a result, we nearly lost my younger brother three times. That experience is the reason I built CroupGuard.

🎙️ Try the Interactive Demo

Tap the button and cough for 6 seconds. The microphone measures loudness and rough pitch right on your device.

🎙️

Live sound from your microphone — it never leaves your device.

This is only a student prototype and not a medical device.

✨ Key Features

🔒Privacy First

Designed with on-device processing and minimal data collection.

🌍Language Access

Supports multiple languages including Ethiopian languages.

💡Simple & Clear

Gives an estimated likelihood so families can decide when to seek care.

⚖️Built for Equity

Made so under-resourced families can access useful information more easily.

📴Works Offline

After the first visit, CroupGuard keeps working even without internet.
